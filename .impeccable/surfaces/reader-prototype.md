# Reader prototype

Mode: Read. This throwaway surface answers how provenance and incomplete processing remain clear while a person finds and reads saved research. The user delegated implementation and prototype choices. Three variants are required by the prototype skill; they are alternatives for comparison, not a production design system.

## Grounded directions

1. Margin notebook: a persistent source list alongside one long research note.
2. Evidence desk: source material and interpretation occupy separate reading fields.
3. Accession ledger: a source register makes availability and review state the primary selection context.
4. Compact library catalog: bibliographic list first, separate document view second.
5. Laboratory report: fixed sections with method and uncertainty before conclusions.
6. Abstract reading room: one source presented with citation and document navigation.
7. Tabbed binder: broad topic partitions above a common document surface.

The concept seed assigned direction 3 (44021343). All first three structures are implemented because the explicit prototype request requires three distinct variations. Direction 3 is therefore the assigned alternate rather than an exclusive production selection.

Challenger judgments: the data field and cutting bench weaken prose comprehension and audience identification; keep their discipline of explicit state. The cloud edge weakens research-task identification; keep clear separation of the evidence field from its surrounding control area. The cape and guide map obscure ordinary references; keep their insistence on consistent navigation. The dark developer console holds panel clarity but loses audience identification; keep predictable responsive collapse. No challenger changes the product facts.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Make provenance legible in ordinary reading. The three structures disagree about whether a reading column, an evidence comparison, or a register should organize the reader.

OWN-WORLD: Cool white paper, blue-gray library surfaces, dark ink, modest blue selection, brown attention states. Source Sans 3 handles controls and structured content; Source Serif 4 distinguishes extended source prose and human commentary.

STORY: Search or filter saved sources, choose one, read the source record, then inspect machine interpretation, its limitations, and personal commentary.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A shows a narrow library margin and a long note; B shows a horizontal source strip above paired evidence fields; C shows a broad source register beside the selected record. Phone layouts stack those structures without shrinking their text.

FORM: The assigned accession ledger is grounded candidate 3, seed 44021343. The notebook and desk are full prototype alternates.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
