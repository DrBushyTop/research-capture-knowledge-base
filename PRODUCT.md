# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Astro static reader, specified in the implementation brief. Prototype design decisions are delegated by the user; this branch is throwaway.

## Users

One person saves research through Zotero on Android and desktop, then finds and reads it later on either device.

## Product Purpose

Keep source facts, machine interpretation, limitations, and human notes understandable within a private personal research corpus.

## Operating Context

Capture happens in Zotero. Processing happens later. The reader presents repository content, static search, and topic, source-type, and publication-year filters. The initial reader requires sign-in.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Research notes use Markdown and JSON. Human notes survive reprocessing. Source artifacts remain private and separate from notes. Publication eligibility is explicit and conservative. This prototype uses synthetic content, has no authentication or persistence, and exercises the reader interface only.

## Evidence on Hand

The implementation brief and CONTEXT.md define product behavior. No production reader or visual identity exists. Every source, citation, and research statement shown in this prototype is labeled synthetic.

## Product Principles

- Make source facts distinguishable from interpretation.
- Expose missing text and review needs where a reader encounters them.
- Keep finding and reading comfortable on phone and desktop.
- Preserve human commentary as human commentary.
