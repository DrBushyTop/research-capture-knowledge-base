## Machine interpretation

A recipe webpage describing techniques for creamy homemade hummus, based on the author's reported ten preparation attempts and adaptations of recipes from established chefs\. Its main technique is boiling cooked chickpeas with baking soda until very soft before blending with tahini, lemon juice, garlic, ice water, cumin and olive oil\. Texture and flavor claims are primarily subjective culinary observations, not findings from a controlled study\.

## Key findings

- The author reports that boiling canned or previously cooked chickpeas with baking soda for about 20 minutes produces softer chickpeas and smoother hummus without individually peeling the skins\.
- The author identifies tahini quality and a ratio of ½ cup tahini to one 15-ounce can of chickpeas as important to the desired flavor and richness\.
- Ice water is reported to make the tahini mixture paler and the hummus lighter and fluffier; the author explicitly states that a scientific explanation was not found\.
- Finely processing garlic with lemon juice and allowing it to rest is reported to reduce harsh garlic flavor\.
- The recipe yields approximately 2 cups of hummus\.
